Our Story

Meet The Partners

Four Friends. One Mission.

The Salad Bar Company was founded in 2025 by four friends with a shared vision: to build a creative agency that truly serves its community. We saw a gap in the market—brands needed partners who understood culture, not just campaigns.

Our founding team brings together decades of experience across advertising, content production, and business operations. Joshua, Corey, and Tarahgee cut their teeth in the fast-paced world of advertising and media production, working with major brands and understanding what makes content resonate. Janel brings a sharp financial acumen and operational excellence from her background in finance, ensuring every project runs smoothly from pitch to delivery.

Together, we mix diverse perspectives into something fresh—creative solutions that are strategic, beautifully executed, and rooted in the communities we serve.
